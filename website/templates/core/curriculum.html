A note on CBC → CBE

In April 2025, Kenya's Ministry of Education reviewed and rebranded CBC as Competency-Based Education (CBE). 2026 is the first year Grade 10 learners select pathways under the new Senior School structure — making this a particularly opportune moment to embed cloud computing content into the emerging STEM track. Cloud for Kids is positioned as complementary enrichment, not a replacement for formal curriculum delivery.
